✈ CAPABILITY CARDS

What I build

Three areas of practice, one craft: turning deep technical work in semiconductors, firmware and AI into products that ship.

01

AI Product System

Defining what gets built at the intersection of silicon and machine intelligence — product strategy, roadmaps, and launches for AI-enabled embedded products.

  • Product strategy and roadmapping for AI-enabled semiconductor solutions
  • Bridging firmware, hardware, and AI/ML capabilities into cohesive products
  • On-device inference and edge AI integrated with existing platforms
  • Partnering across engineering, design, and business stakeholders
02

Firmware Platform

The low-level foundation: real-time firmware, connectivity, and security on resource-constrained semiconductor platforms — from first silicon to deployed devices.

  • Device drivers, bootloaders, and interrupt-driven real-time control
  • Silicon bring-up, register mapping, and hardware validation
  • BLE, Wi-Fi, and Ethernet communication layers for multi-OS clients
  • Secure boot, firmware signing, and over-the-air update mechanisms
03

Business System

The connective tissue: translating technical possibility into prioritized, resourced, shipped outcomes — with the data and judgment to back each call.

  • Feature definition and prioritization with engineering and hardware teams
  • Data-driven roadmapping and AI-assisted prototyping to test ideas fast
  • Architectures for connected products, from device to gateway to cloud
  • Shipping discipline: useful, understandable, production-ready systems
